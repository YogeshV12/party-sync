Privacy Policy

Crowd Speaker for iOS and Android · Effective October 14, 2026

In short: Crowd Speaker has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no servers. We do not collect, store or receive any of your data. Your voice travels only over your local Wi-Fi, directly between the phones in your own session, and is never recorded.

Who we are

Crowd Speaker is published by CLEANDOT ENTERPRISES PRIVATE LIMITED ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy: yogeshk.verma97@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not require an account or sign-in, and it contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software. We operate no server that the app talks to, so we never receive anything from your device.

How your voice is handled

  • When you choose "Be the mic", the app captures your voice from the microphone and streams it live to the other phones you invited.
  • That audio is sent only over the local Wi-Fi network or hotspot that your phones share, between your own devices. It is not sent over the internet and not sent to us or any third party.
  • Audio is played and then discarded. It is never recorded or saved, on the host or on the listening phones.

Permissions the app asks for

PermissionWhy
MicrophoneOnly while you are the mic, to capture your voice for live playback on the other phones.
CameraOnly to scan the host's QR code when joining. Images are processed on the device to read the code and are never saved or sent anywhere.
Local network / Wi-Fi accessTo send and receive audio and timing signals between phones on the same Wi-Fi, and to join the host's Wi-Fi from inside the app.
Notifications and background activity (Android)To keep a live session running with the screen off and to show that it is active.
Location (Android 12L and older only)Required by those Android versions for Wi-Fi Direct device discovery. The app does not read, store or transmit your location.

You can change or revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings. The app will not be able to host or join a session without the permissions it needs for that.

Data stored on your device

The app saves a few settings locally on your phone so you do not have to re-enter them: your display name, an optional hotspot name and password, and audio-sync adjustment values. This stays on your device, is not transmitted to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, share or disclose personal information, because we do not have any. The app includes no third-party SDKs that collect data. In a session, what other participants can see is limited to what the session needs, such as your chosen display name and connection status.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Security

Because nothing is sent to our servers, there is no account or database of yours for us to protect. Session audio travels only on the local network you choose to use; as with any local Wi-Fi activity, use a network you trust.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
